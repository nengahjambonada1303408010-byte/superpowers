# Skill: Market Session Analysis — XAUUSD

## Purpose
Identify optimal trading windows for XAUUSD scalping based on global market session activity.

## Session Schedule (GMT)

### Asian Session (00:00 - 07:00 GMT)
- **Volume:** Low
- **Volatility:** Low — XAUUSD typically ranges 20-40 pts
- **Strategy:** Range trading only. Fade the extremes.
- **Scalping:** NOT recommended. High spread, choppy movement.
- **Exception:** If Asia forms tight consolidation, breakout at 06:00-07:00 can be traded

### London Open (07:00 - 09:00 GMT) ★ PRIME TIME
- **Volume:** Very High
- **Volatility:** High — often 50-100+ pts moves
- **Strategy:** Trade the London breakout of Asia range
- **Best setup:** Price breaks above/below Asia high/low with volume spike
- **Scalping:** Highly recommended. Best spreads, strong directional moves.

### London Midday (09:00 - 12:00 GMT)
- **Volume:** High
- **Volatility:** Moderate-High
- **Strategy:** Trend continuation after London open direction established
- **Scalping:** Good. Trend trades work well.

### NY Open (13:30 - 15:30 GMT) ★ PRIME TIME
- **Volume:** Very High (US + London overlap)
- **Volatility:** Highest of day
- **Strategy:** Follow London trend OR trade reversal if London was one-sided
- **Scalping:** Excellent. Maximum liquidity, tight spreads.

### NY Afternoon (15:30 - 17:00 GMT)
- **Volume:** Moderate
- **Volatility:** Decreasing
- **Strategy:** Close most positions. Avoid new entries after 16:30.

### NY Close (17:00+ GMT)
- **Action:** Close all scalping positions. Do not hold overnight scalps.

## High-Impact News (Avoid Trading 30 min Before/After)
- US CPI, PPI, NFP (Non-Farm Payrolls)
- Fed Interest Rate Decision
- FOMC Meeting Minutes
- US GDP
- Geopolitical events causing gold safe-haven demand
